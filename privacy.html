Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Sampsons Window Services ("we", "us") cleans windows and washes homes in Kyle, TX and the surrounding area. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect the information you give us when you request a quote, book a service, or talk with us in person, including at your door: your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the work you want done.

How we use your information

  • To give you quotes and schedule, confirm and remind you about appointments
  • To answer your questions and provide customer service
  • To send invoices and receipts

Text messages

When you share your phone number with us, whether through our website, by calling or texting us, or in person, you agree to receive text messages from Sampsons Window Services about your quotes and appointments. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. You do not have to agree to texts to buy our services.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Who we share information with

We only share your information with service providers that help us run our business (such as our scheduling, texting and payment software), and only so they can provide those services to us. We may also share information when the law requires it.

How we protect your information

We keep your information in secure, password-protected business software and only use it to serve you.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by calling, texting or emailing us. Reply STOP to any text to opt out of messages.
